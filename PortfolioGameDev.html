About me!

Hello! I'm Bogdan Cristea, a Video Game Programming graduate from Lasalle College in Quebec Canada.

My journey into game development started early—at 14, I created my first prototype using RPG Maker MV, a small fan project for Markiplier that ignited my passion for game creation. That early spark drove me to pursue formal education and master industry-standard engines like Unity (my current favorite) and Unreal Engine.

Beyond game development, I've expanded my programming toolkit to include web technologies like CSS, HTML, and React—skills I sharpened during a 3-month front-end internship at Ozex, where I contributed to their mobile app development.

Throughout my career, I've had the opportunity to work on diverse projects across 2D, 3D, and VR, each reinforcing my love for crafting immersive stories. I thrive on building narratives that players can truly lose themselves in—a passion that extends beyond games into tabletop roleplaying like Dungeons & Dragons, where I'm known for going all-in on storytelling and narrative hooks.

What excites me most? Problem-solving and creativity. Whether I'm debugging code, designing a new game mechanic, or building a web application, I'm always eager to create something meaningful.
